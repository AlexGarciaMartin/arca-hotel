## Prompt maestro

Photorealistic luxury architectural photography, wide horizontal, no text, logos or people. One large sophisticated Svalbard resort, not scattered cabins: a long charcoal concrete building stepped into a bare rocky hillside, partially buried service level, main level two stories above, projecting pale laminated timber Aurora Lounge with a monumental curved glass wall facing the calm inlet. Higher rear suite wing with flat roof and a very slender spire bearing a tiny red tip light. Glacier descending to the left when looking from the hotel towards the inlet. Private pier, small unloading crane and helipad below. Arctic summer midnight light, desaturated stone and ice blue, warm timber and interior lighting. Believable high-end hospitality photography.
